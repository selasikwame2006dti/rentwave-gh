RentWave GH - Logo Concepts

Five SVG marks on the brand palette: navy #0b2239, wave teal-blue gradient, Ghana gold #ffb703.

A · Roof + Swell — "the safe pick"

White roof chevron over a W drawn as a wave crest. Gold foam line = Ghana sunshine on water.

B · The Wave-W — "the modern pick"

A teal W drawn as two crossing ocean swells under a navy roofline, with a gold sun dot. Light background — reads great on white headers.

C · House in Wave — "the bold pick"

A white home riding a navy swell with a gold sun. Simplest silhouette — strongest at tiny sizes (favicon, app icon).

D · The Key — "unlock your home"

House-shaped keyhole with a gold key ring orbiting it — the unlock moment. Wave breathes below. Distinct from every GH competitor (they're all generic blue corporate).

E · W-House Monogram — "the clever pick"

One bold W whose centre peak IS a roofline (teal chevron), gold sun in the valley, wave underline. Letterform + story in one stroke — scales from favicon to billboard.
