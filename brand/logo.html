Logo Concepts — pick a direction 🎨

Three hand-coded SVG marks. All share one idea: the wave is the brand — competitors (meQasa, Jiji, GPC) all use generic houses; nobody owns the wave. Each concept is tested on light + navy, and at favicon size.

The three marks

Concept A

The Wave Roof

A home whose roofline is a living wave — the whole brand story in one stroke. Open line-art keeps it friendly and modern; the gold sun adds Ghana warmth.

🏠 Trust🌊 Motion☀️ Warmth
Concept B

Wave Ring Home

A solid home wrapped in a wave that never closes — like water circling the globe. The open ring says "movement, flow, nationwide." Filled house = security. Strongest app-icon candidate.

🔒 Secure🌍 Nationwide📱 Icon-first
Concept C

Pin & Home

A map pin with a home carved out of the middle — every listing is GPS-pinned, search is location-first. Most literal "find a home here" mark. The safest, most instant read.

📍 GPS-first🔍 Search✅ Instant read
